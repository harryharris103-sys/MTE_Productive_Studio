Tool options

Brush

Spacing, scatter, jitter and follow apply to stamp brushes (Soft stamp, Chalk, Splatter, Dry bristle and imported tips). Each stroke becomes its own layer.

Paint bucket

Click the canvas. The colour is the A colour in the Fill section. A selection limits the fill.

Quick selection

Like Photoshop's Quick Selection: brush over the part you want and the selection grows out to the edges of similar colour. Use Subtract to brush parts back out; untick Auto-expand for a plain selection brush. Pick the layer in Layers before deleting pixels.

Object selection

Like Photoshop's Object Selection: drag a rectangle or lasso loosely around the object and it finds the edges inside. A plain click picks the similar-coloured area (hair, clothes, eyes). Use Add or Subtract to refine. It works from colour and edges and runs offline.

Eraser

Rubs out pixels of the picked layer so the background shows through (text and shapes are turned into pixels). Stays inside a selection if you have one.

Shape borders

Rectangles round natively. Triangles and polygons become editable paths when rounded. Ellipses have no corners.

Curves

Click the curve to add a point, drag to shape it, double-click a point to remove it. The layer changes everything below it and can be hidden or deleted at any time.

Curvature pen

Click to add points; the path curves smoothly through them. Drag a point to move it, double-click it for a sharp corner, click the first point to close, Delete removes a point, Enter finishes. Use Edit points later to fine-tune.

Blur tool

Brush: paint on the picked layer (it stays inside a marquee selection if you have one). Whole layer / Selected area blur in one go with Apply blur.

Layer mask

Layers

Layers

Drag the ≡ handle to reorder layers. Ctrl, Cmd or Alt + click adds or removes a layer. Shift + click selects from one layer to another. Ctrl+A selects all layers.
Appearance

Fill & stroke

Layer opacity

Opacity fades the whole layer, effects included. Fill fades only the colour of shapes and text; borders stay solid.

Effects & blending

Filters

Works on images and any other layer, can be changed or removed at any time, and shows in exports. Select several layers to filter them together. Not available inside groups.
Type & layout

Text

Align

Opens SVG, PSD, PDF, AI, images and .mte projects. Drop files onto the canvas. Arrow keys nudge (Shift = 10px).

New project

Custom size

Layer Style (FX)

Effects are non-destructive: untick one to hide it, change it any time, or remove all. They follow the layer when you move or scale it. Not available on layers inside a group.
